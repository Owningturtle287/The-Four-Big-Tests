# Political Compass 101: item revision log

Version 2.0. All 101 original IDs, facets, and signed weights are retained. Every statement has been reviewed and rewritten. Numerical thresholds describe hypothetical policies; they are not factual claims or empirically validated cutoffs.

Changes make the actor, action, scope, exception, or tradeoff explicit. Some scenarios narrow the original construct substantially. The revised instrument therefore requires fresh validation. Original paired-item coherence is intentionally not presented as a reliability statistic: two context-dependent policy preferences need not agree.

The result describes economic and governance preferences, not party membership. Original weighting choices are heuristic. Unanswered/context-dependent responses are omitted from the denominator; neutral responses remain zero. Scores are withheld when weighted coverage of either axis is below 70%.

## Q001 · E1a · E1

**Original:** Essential network utilities, such as water or electricity, should usually be publicly owned.

**Revised:** Water distribution networks should be owned by a public authority rather than private shareholders, with charges covering maintenance and operating costs.

## Q002 · A1a · A1

**Original:** Government should not punish peaceful speech merely because many people find it offensive.

**Revised:** Publishing an opinion that insults a religion should remain legal when it contains no threat, incitement to violence, or targeted harassment.

## Q003 · E2a · E2

**Original:** Higher-income households should pay a larger percentage of their income in taxes than middle-income households.

**Revised:** A household earning five times the national median income should pay a higher effective income-tax rate than a household earning the median.

## Q004 · A2a · A2

**Original:** Except in an immediate danger, police should need a judicial warrant to access a person's private digital messages.

**Revised:** Police investigating a crime should obtain a warrant from an independent judge before reading a suspect's private messages, except to prevent an imminent threat to life.

## Q005 · E3a · E3

**Original:** Employees should be able to form a union without interference from their employer.

**Revised:** An employer should be prohibited from dismissing or demoting a worker for organizing a lawful union, even when the employer opposes unionization.

## Q006 · A3a · A3

**Original:** Adults should generally be free to make personal choices unless those choices directly harm another person.

**Revised:** Adults should be allowed to sell sexually explicit material to consenting adults, with safeguards against coercion and access by children.

## Q007 · E4a · E4

**Original:** A universal public health-insurance plan should guarantee a core package of care to everyone.

**Revised:** Every resident should receive medically necessary hospital and primary care through a tax-funded public insurance plan, even if this increases taxes.

## Q008 · A4a · A4

**Original:** An executive leader should need legislative authorization to continue emergency powers beyond a short initial period.

**Revised:** Emergency powers that bypass ordinary lawmaking should expire after 30 days unless the legislature votes to renew them.

## Q009 · E5a · E5

**Original:** Environmental rules should require firms to bear measurable costs that their activity imposes on the public.

**Revised:** A factory should pay for the independently measured air-pollution damage it causes, even when that raises its production costs.

## Q010 · A5a · A5

**Original:** Public decisions should be made at the lowest level of government that can manage their consequences.

**Revised:** Municipalities should set land-use rules when the effects stay within their boundaries, even if this produces different rules between neighboring towns.

## Q011 · E6a · E6

**Original:** Free-trade agreements should generally be favored even when some domestic industries become smaller.

**Revised:** Import tariffs should be removed when this lowers consumer prices overall, even if the government expects some domestic factories to close.

## Q012 · A6a · A6

**Original:** Government should need prompt judicial review before placing a citizen under a security-based travel restriction.

**Revised:** A citizen placed on a security no-fly list should be able to challenge the restriction before an independent judge within 14 days.

## Q013 · E3b · E3

**Original:** Employers and individual workers should generally be free to negotiate terms without sector-wide bargaining requirements.

**Revised:** Employers and workers should be allowed to agree on pay outside an industry-wide union contract, provided they meet statutory labor protections.

## Q014 · A2b · A2

**Original:** Broad monitoring of public communications is acceptable for preventing terrorism when a secret court provides oversight.

**Revised:** A security agency should be allowed to collect and automatically analyze everyone's public social-media posts for terrorism risks, with oversight from a court whose proceedings are secret.

## Q015 · E2b · E2

**Original:** Taxes should be low and broadly uniform even if this results in less redistribution.

**Revised:** All households should pay the same percentage of income above a basic tax-free allowance, even if this redistributes less income than higher rates for higher earners.

## Q016 · A1b · A1

**Original:** Speech that seriously undermines social cohesion may be restricted even when it does not directly threaten violence.

**Revised:** Government should be allowed to prohibit peaceful political speech that it judges likely to increase hostility between social groups, even without a threat or incitement to violence.

## Q017 · E1b · E1

**Original:** Privately owned firms should remain the normal form of ownership in most industries where meaningful competition is possible.

**Revised:** In industries where several firms can compete without natural monopoly, businesses should normally be owned by private investors rather than the government.

## Q018 · X1 · X

**Original:** An employer should be barred from firing a worker for lawful political activity outside work unless the activity materially affects the job.

**Revised:** An employer should be prohibited from firing someone for attending a lawful political rally outside work when it does not affect their job performance or workplace conduct.

## Q019 · A6b · A6

**Original:** Citizenship may be revoked from a dual national who is convicted of a serious terrorism offense.

**Revised:** A court should be able to revoke citizenship from a dual national convicted of a terrorism offense that caused deaths, provided the person retains another citizenship.

## Q020 · E6b · E6

**Original:** Government should favor domestic suppliers in order to preserve industries it considers strategically important.

**Revised:** Government procurement should favor domestic semiconductor manufacturers over cheaper foreign suppliers to preserve domestic production capacity.

## Q021 · A5b · A5

**Original:** National government should be able to override local rules in order to maintain uniform standards across the country.

**Revised:** The national government should be able to impose the same minimum building-safety rules on every municipality, even when local voters prefer weaker rules.

## Q022 · E5b · E5

**Original:** When either approach can meet the same environmental target, pricing pollution is preferable to prescribing production methods.

**Revised:** If independent evidence shows two policies would reduce emissions equally, government should charge firms for their emissions rather than require a particular production technology.

## Q023 · A4b · A4

**Original:** During a prolonged crisis, decisive executive action can be more important than obtaining advance legislative approval.

**Revised:** During a declared emergency lasting more than 30 days, the executive should be able to extend its extraordinary powers without a legislative vote if it believes delay would worsen the crisis.

## Q024 · E4b · E4

**Original:** Competition among private insurers should be the main organizing principle for health coverage.

**Revised:** Residents should normally obtain basic health coverage from competing private insurers, with public subsidies for those who cannot afford premiums.

## Q025 · A3b · A3

**Original:** Government may restrict adult conduct judged seriously immoral even when direct harm to another person has not been shown.

**Revised:** Government should be allowed to prohibit consensual sexual relationships between unrelated adults solely because the majority considers those relationships immoral.

## Q026 · E6c · E6

**Original:** Capital gains should be taxed at a lower rate than wages in order to encourage investment.

**Revised:** Profits from selling investments held for more than a year should be taxed at a lower rate than wages of the same amount.

## Q027 · A6c · A6

**Original:** Military service should remain voluntary except when the country faces a direct threat to its survival.

**Revised:** Military service should be voluntary unless the country faces an invasion that threatens its continued existence.

## Q028 · E1c · E1

**Original:** Employee-owned enterprises should receive limited tax advantages over otherwise similar investor-owned firms.

**Revised:** An employee-owned business should receive a lower corporate tax rate than an otherwise identical investor-owned business.

## Q029 · A1c · A1

**Original:** People should be allowed to publish political arguments anonymously.

**Revised:** People should be allowed to publish political opinions under a pseudonym without registering their real identity with the government.

## Q030 · E2c · E2

**Original:** A tax on very large inheritances is a legitimate way to limit the concentration of wealth across generations.

**Revised:** The portion of an inheritance exceeding 100 times the national median annual income should be subject to a separate inheritance tax.

## Q031 · A2c · A2

**Original:** Evidence obtained through a serious illegal search should generally be excluded from a criminal trial even when it indicates guilt.

**Revised:** Evidence obtained when police deliberately search a home without a required warrant should be excluded from a criminal trial, even if it proves guilt.

## Q032 · E3c · E3

**Original:** A statutory minimum wage should be high enough to support a basic standard of living even if some low-wage jobs disappear.

**Revised:** The legal minimum wage for full-time work should cover a locally defined basic living budget, even if credible estimates predict a small reduction in low-wage employment.

## Q033 · A3c · A3

**Original:** Same-sex couples should have the same access to civil marriage as opposite-sex couples.

**Revised:** Two adults of the same sex should have the same legal right to civil marriage as two adults of different sexes.

## Q034 · E4c · E4

**Original:** Public funding for primary and secondary education should be used to reduce large quality gaps between neighborhoods.

**Revised:** Public schools in low-income neighborhoods should receive additional funding to narrow measured resource gaps with schools in wealthy neighborhoods.

## Q035 · A4c · A4

**Original:** Courts should be able to invalidate laws that violate constitutional rights even when those laws were approved by an elected majority.

**Revised:** An independent constitutional court should be able to strike down a law that violates constitutional rights, even when the law has majority voter support.

## Q036 · E5c · E5

**Original:** Regulators may restrict a new product when credible evidence points to severe harm even though uncertainty remains.

**Revised:** A regulator should be able to temporarily suspend sales of a product when credible evidence suggests it may cause fatal illness, while further testing establishes the size of the risk.

## Q037 · X2 · X

**Original:** A dominant digital platform should be required to give users a clear appeal process when it removes political speech.

**Revised:** A social-media platform used by most adults should provide notice and a human-reviewed appeal when it removes lawful political speech for breaking its rules.

## Q038 · A5c · A5

**Original:** Citizens should be able to place a proposal on a binding referendum after meeting a substantial signature requirement.

**Revised:** Citizens should be able to trigger a binding national referendum by collecting verified signatures from 5% of eligible voters, subject to constitutional review.

## Q039 · E2d · E2

**Original:** People should generally be free to transfer accumulated wealth to their heirs without a special inheritance tax.

**Revised:** Wealth transferred at death should face no separate inheritance tax, even when an estate exceeds 100 times the national median annual income.

## Q040 · A1d · A1

**Original:** Organizers of large public demonstrations should need prior government permission rather than merely give notice.

**Revised:** Organizers of a peaceful march that uses public streets should need government approval of the event, rather than only notifying officials so traffic can be managed.

## Q041 · E1d · E1

**Original:** Government should be allowed to create a public enterprise when a private market repeatedly fails to provide an essential service.

**Revised:** Government should establish a publicly owned provider when a region has repeatedly been left without reliable broadband because private firms find it unprofitable.

## Q042 · A6d · A6

**Original:** Most young adults should be required to complete a period of military or civilian national service.

**Revised:** Every young adult should complete six months of military or civilian public service, with exemptions for disability and substantial caregiving responsibilities.

## Q043 · E6d · E6

**Original:** A public development bank should finance socially useful projects that private investors consider too risky.

**Revised:** A publicly owned development bank should fund long-term infrastructure projects with substantial public benefits even when commercial banks reject them as too risky.

## Q044 · A5d · A5

**Original:** Elected representatives should make final policy choices rather than rely frequently on referendums.

**Revised:** Elected legislators should make final decisions on ordinary national laws instead of allowing those laws to be decided by binding citizen-initiated referendums.

## Q045 · E5d · E5

**Original:** Firms should be free to sell a legal product unless regulators can demonstrate that it is likely to cause significant harm.

**Revised:** A business should be allowed to continue selling a new product while regulators investigate possible harm, until evidence shows that significant harm is likely.

## Q046 · A4d · A4

**Original:** Except where constitutional text is explicit, elected majorities should be able to implement policy without judges overturning it.

**Revised:** When a constitution does not explicitly protect the right at issue, judges should defer to an elected legislature rather than invalidate its law using broader constitutional principles.

## Q047 · E4d · E4

**Original:** Families should be able to direct public education funding to an approved private school of their choice.

**Revised:** Parents should be allowed to transfer their child's public-school funding to a licensed private school that meets the same academic and admissions standards.

## Q048 · A3d · A3

**Original:** A court should be able to authorize lifesaving treatment for a competent adult who refuses it when death is otherwise imminent.

**Revised:** A court should be able to order a lifesaving blood transfusion for a competent adult who understands the consequences and refuses it for personal beliefs.

## Q049 · E3d · E3

**Original:** Legal wage floors should mainly reflect local labor-market conditions rather than a uniform national standard.

**Revised:** Regional governments should set minimum wages according to local wages and living costs, without having to meet a single national wage floor.

## Q050 · A2d · A2

**Original:** Courts should give police wider discretion over searches when officials can show a substantial public-safety risk.

**Revised:** When police have specific evidence of an imminent armed attack, they should be allowed to search a suspect's home without waiting for a warrant, with judicial review afterward.

## Q051 · E5e · E5

**Original:** Consumer-protection rules should standardize important contract terms even when this limits the variety of products offered.

**Revised:** All consumer loans should use legally standardized rules for fees and early repayment, even if this prevents lenders from offering some customized contracts.

## Q052 · A5e · A5

**Original:** Minor political parties should receive legislative representation roughly in proportion to their share of the vote.

**Revised:** A party receiving 10% of votes in a national legislative election should receive about 10% of seats, even if coalition governments become more common.

## Q053 · E6e · E6

**Original:** Private banks should allocate most business credit without government targets for particular industries.

**Revised:** Private banks should decide which lawful businesses receive loans without government requirements to lend a specified share to favored industries.

## Q054 · A6e · A6

**Original:** Each asylum claim should receive an individualized hearing even when the number of arrivals is unusually high.

**Revised:** Each person requesting asylum should receive an individual assessment and a chance to appeal rejection, even during a large surge in arrivals.

## Q055 · E1e · E1

**Original:** Subject to contracts and general law, profitable firms should decide for themselves how to divide earnings among owners, workers, and reinvestment.

**Revised:** After meeting contracts, taxes, and minimum employment standards, a profitable company should decide how much of its remaining profit goes to shareholders rather than employees.

## Q056 · X3 · X

**Original:** During a declared national emergency, government may direct private production of essential goods for a limited period subject to legislative renewal.

**Revised:** During a declared pandemic, government should be able to require factories to produce essential medical equipment at compensated rates for up to 90 days, with any extension requiring a legislative vote.

## Q057 · A1e · A1

**Original:** A peaceful organization should not be banned solely because it rejects the existing constitutional order.

**Revised:** A peaceful organization that campaigns to replace democracy with a monarchy should remain legal as long as it does not plan or incite violence.

## Q058 · E2e · E2

**Original:** Government should guarantee every adult a minimum income floor, even if doing so requires higher taxes.

**Revised:** Government should guarantee every adult an income sufficient for a basic living budget through payments that decrease as earnings rise, even if this requires higher taxes.

## Q059 · A2e · A2

**Original:** A person in police custody should have access to a lawyer before substantive questioning begins.

**Revised:** A person arrested on suspicion of a crime should be able to consult a lawyer before police question them about the alleged offense.

## Q060 · E3e · E3

**Original:** Workers should elect representatives to the boards of large companies.

**Revised:** Employees should elect one-third of the board members of large companies, even when employees do not own shares.

## Q061 · A3e · A3

**Original:** During a serious outbreak of a contagious disease, government may require vaccination for access to crowded public settings.

**Revised:** During an outbreak of a potentially fatal contagious disease, entry to crowded indoor public venues should require vaccination when strong evidence shows it substantially reduces transmission, with medical exemptions.

## Q062 · E4e · E4

**Original:** A public pension should guarantee a basic retirement benefit that does not depend on financial-market performance.

**Revised:** A tax-funded public pension should guarantee a basic retirement income regardless of how investments perform.

## Q063 · A4e · A4

**Original:** Election administration should be institutionally independent from the government currently in office.

**Revised:** The officials who administer national elections should be appointed through a process requiring agreement from both governing and opposition parties, rather than by the government alone.

## Q064 · E1f · E1

**Original:** When a competitive private market can provide the same service, government should generally sell businesses it owns.

**Revised:** Government should sell a business it owns when a competitive private market can provide the same service at a similar price and quality.

## Q065 · A6f · A6

**Original:** Government may detain people who cross a border without authorization while their identities and legal claims are resolved.

**Revised:** An adult who crosses a border without authorization should be detained until their identity and claim are decided, even when authorities have found no individual flight or safety risk.

## Q066 · E6f · E6

**Original:** Vacant urban land held mainly for speculation should face higher taxes than land in active use.

**Revised:** Urban land left unused for more than two years, despite permission to develop it, should face a higher property-tax rate than comparable land in active use.

## Q067 · A5f · A5

**Original:** Electoral rules should favor stable governing majorities even when small parties receive fewer seats than their vote share would imply.

**Revised:** Election rules should give the largest party a seat bonus to make a stable government more likely, even if smaller parties receive fewer seats than their vote shares justify.

## Q068 · E5f · E5

**Original:** Well-informed adults should be able to accept greater financial risk in exchange for fewer regulatory protections.

**Revised:** Adults who pass a financial-knowledge assessment should be allowed to buy high-risk investments that ordinary consumer-protection rules would prohibit.

## Q069 · A4f · A4

**Original:** Voting in national elections should be compulsory, with a modest penalty for unjustified nonparticipation.

**Revised:** Eligible citizens should have to attend or return a ballot in national elections, with a small fine for nonparticipation without a valid excuse; submitting a blank ballot would remain allowed.

## Q070 · E4f · E4

**Original:** Retirement saving should rely mainly on accounts owned and invested by individuals.

**Revised:** Retirement income should come mainly from individually owned investment accounts rather than a tax-funded pension guaranteeing a fixed basic benefit.

## Q071 · A3f · A3

**Original:** Parents should have broad discretion over a child's education provided that minimum academic standards are met.

**Revised:** Parents should be allowed to homeschool their children when independent assessments show the children meet minimum academic standards.

## Q072 · E3f · E3

**Original:** After a lawful strike has continued for a reasonable period, a firm should be allowed to hire permanent replacement workers.

**Revised:** An employer should be allowed to permanently replace workers after a lawful strike has lasted 30 days, even if the strike is continuing.

## Q073 · A2f · A2

**Original:** A court should be able to impose preventive restrictions after a sentence ends when a repeat serious offender is judged highly dangerous.

**Revised:** After a person completes a prison sentence, a court should be able to impose electronic monitoring based on evidence of a high risk of serious reoffending, with regular review and access to appeal.

## Q074 · E2f · E2

**Original:** Public income support should be tightly targeted to people who cannot meet basic needs rather than offered broadly.

**Revised:** Cash income support should be limited to households below a basic-needs threshold rather than paid to households at all income levels.

## Q075 · X4 · X

**Original:** A private employer should generally be free to enforce a workplace dress code even when an employee objects on religious grounds.

**Revised:** An employer should be allowed to ban religious headwear under a general uniform policy even when the headwear creates no safety problem and allowing it would cost little.

## Q076 · A1f · A1

**Original:** Government may prohibit an organization that advocates replacing democracy even when the group is not planning violence.

**Revised:** Government should be allowed to ban a political organization that seeks to end multiparty elections, even if it pursues its goals peacefully and lawfully.

## Q077 · E4g · E4

**Original:** Unemployment benefits should replace a meaningful share of lost wages even if that requires higher payroll taxes.

**Revised:** Unemployment insurance should replace at least half of previous wages for six months, subject to a cap and active job search, even if payroll taxes must rise.

## Q078 · A4g · A4

**Original:** Legislative oversight bodies should have access to national-security operations even when some details must remain confidential.

**Revised:** A security-cleared legislative committee should be able to inspect national-security operations in confidential hearings, even when the executive objects.

## Q079 · E5g · E5

**Original:** During a severe shortage, public agencies should be able to set temporary maximum prices for essential goods.

**Revised:** During a declared disaster, public agencies should be able to temporarily cap the retail price of basic drinking water, even if higher prices might attract additional suppliers.

## Q080 · A5g · A5

**Original:** Public institutions should accommodate minority cultural or religious practices unless doing so creates a substantial burden on others.

**Revised:** A public school should permit religious clothing that does not interfere with safety or teaching, even if its usual uniform rules would prohibit it.

## Q081 · E6g · E6

**Original:** On a new lease, a landlord should generally be free to set rent according to market demand.

**Revised:** When signing a new lease, a landlord should be free to set the rent at any price a tenant accepts, without a government rent cap.

## Q082 · A6g · A6

**Original:** A person who reveals an unlawful national-security program should be allowed to raise a public-interest defense in court.

**Revised:** A person prosecuted for exposing an unlawful security program should be allowed to argue in court that the public benefit justified the disclosure, without an automatic guarantee of acquittal.

## Q083 · E1g · E1

**Original:** A high concentration of private ownership can justify public intervention even when current consumer prices are low.

**Revised:** Government should be able to require divestment when one private group controls most major news outlets, even if its prices to consumers remain low.

## Q084 · A1g · A1

**Original:** Journalists should be legally free to publish classified information of public importance unless a court finds a specific and serious risk of harm.

**Revised:** Journalists should be allowed to publish evidence of government misconduct found in classified documents unless a court identifies a specific, serious threat to people's safety.

## Q085 · E2g · E2

**Original:** Reducing large differences in disposable income is a legitimate goal of tax policy, even when basic needs are already met.

**Revised:** Reducing large differences in after-tax income should remain a goal of tax policy even after every household can meet its basic needs.

## Q086 · A2g · A2

**Original:** A low-risk defendant should not remain in jail before trial solely because the defendant cannot afford cash bail.

**Revised:** A defendant whom a judge finds unlikely to flee or harm others should not stay in jail before trial solely because they cannot afford a cash deposit.

## Q087 · E3g · E3

**Original:** Contract workers who depend economically on one platform should receive protections similar to those of employees.

**Revised:** A delivery worker who gets nearly all their income from one platform and must follow its pricing rules should receive employee-like minimum-pay and injury protections.

## Q088 · A3g · A3

**Original:** All schools should be required to teach a common civic curriculum in addition to basic academic subjects.

**Revised:** All schools, including private and home schools, should teach a national course on constitutional institutions and citizens' legal rights alongside basic academic subjects.

## Q089 · E6h · E6

**Original:** A dominant firm should be broken up when its market power persistently blocks new competitors, even if its prices remain low.

**Revised:** A dominant company should be split into separate businesses when it repeatedly blocks competitors from entering its market, even if current prices are low.

## Q090 · A5h · A5

**Original:** National institutions should apply uniform rules rather than create separate accommodations for cultural or religious groups.

**Revised:** Public schools should apply the same dress rules to everyone rather than grant religious exemptions, even when an exemption would create no safety problem and cost little.

## Q091 · E5h · E5

**Original:** During a shortage, prices should generally be allowed to rise unless there is evidence of collusion or deception.

**Revised:** During a temporary shortage, sellers should be allowed to raise prices for essential goods in response to demand, provided they do not collude or deceive buyers.

## Q092 · A4h · A4

**Original:** An elected executive should be able to dismiss senior civil servants in order to carry out its policy program.

**Revised:** An elected national leader should be able to dismiss senior career civil servants for refusing to support the leader's lawful policy program, without having to prove professional misconduct.

## Q093 · E4h · E4

**Original:** Adults should normally purchase non-emergency services themselves rather than receive them through universal public programs.

**Revised:** Adults should pay for routine dental care themselves or through private insurance, with targeted help for low-income patients, rather than receive it through a universal public program.

## Q094 · X5 · X

**Original:** A local government should be free to suspend a national business regulation it considers unnecessary when the effects remain within its borders.

**Revised:** A municipality should be allowed to suspend a national shop-opening-hours rule when the effects of doing so remain within its boundaries.

## Q095 · A3h · A3

**Original:** Possession of a small amount of a recreational drug for personal use should be handled as a health or civil matter rather than a crime.

**Revised:** Possession of a small quantity of a recreational drug for personal use should lead to a civil or health response rather than a criminal conviction; sale to others would remain separately regulated.

## Q096 · E3h · E3

**Original:** Rules on overtime should usually be left to contracts unless a clear health or safety risk is involved.

**Revised:** Employees and employers should be allowed to agree on overtime pay without a statutory overtime premium, provided working hours meet health and safety limits.

## Q097 · A2h · A2

**Original:** When rehabilitation and secure confinement conflict, prisons should give greater priority to confinement.

**Revised:** When a fixed prison budget cannot fund both, spending should prioritize preventing escapes over education and treatment programs intended to reduce reoffending.

## Q098 · E2h · E2

**Original:** The tax system should prioritize incentives for investment over reducing differences in after-tax income.

**Revised:** When two tax reforms raise the same revenue, government should prefer the one expected to increase investment over the one expected to reduce after-tax income inequality.

## Q099 · A1h · A1

**Original:** An elected government should be able to set the editorial priorities of publicly funded broadcasters.

**Revised:** The elected government should be allowed to direct which political issues a publicly funded broadcaster prioritizes in its news coverage.

## Q100 · E1h · E1

**Original:** The prospect of unequal ownership is an acceptable consequence of allowing people to invest and build businesses.

**Revised:** Large differences in business ownership are acceptable when they arise from lawful investment and entrepreneurship, even if that concentrates substantial economic influence.

## Q101 · A6h · A6

**Original:** A security agency should be allowed to withhold some operational programs from legislative committees when disclosure could compromise them.

**Revised:** A security agency should be allowed to conceal an operation even from a security-cleared legislative oversight committee when the agency believes disclosure could endanger it.
